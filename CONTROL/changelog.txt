1.0.0 (2026-10-14)
- Initial public release
- Mobile web app for expenses and income: Home, Transactions, Review, Plan, Budgets and Settings
- Imports bank notification emails from Gmail, Outlook / Microsoft 365 (OAuth), IMAP or POP3 mailboxes, with encrypted credentials
- Categorization by rules, per-merchant memory and Naive Bayes; recurring transactions; budgets
- Multi-currency (CLP, USD, EUR, UF) with official rates from mindicador.cl
- Several independent users, each with their own database
- English and Spanish, chosen in Settings > Appearance
